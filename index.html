Uploadz

Upload and publish your videos to TikTok from one place

Uploadz lets creators connect their TikTok account with Login Kit, pick a video they made, write the caption, choose the privacy level and the comment, duet and stitch settings, and publish it to their own account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account with "Continue with TikTok".
  2. Upload a video file from your computer.
  3. Review your account, write the caption and choose who can view the video and how others can interact with it.
  4. Publish. The video is sent to TikTok and appears on your profile once TikTok finishes processing it.
