Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Uploadz ("the Service") handles when you connect your TikTok account and publish videos, and how it is used.

1. Information we receive

  • From TikTok, with your authorisation: your TikTok open ID, display name, username and avatar (user.info.basic, user.info.profile), your follower, following, like and video counts (user.info.stats), a list of your recent public videos (video.list), and the access and refresh tokens that let the Service act for you.
  • Content you provide: the video files you upload and the caption, privacy level and interaction settings you choose.

We do not collect your TikTok password. Login happens on TikTok's own pages.

2. How we use it

  • To show you which TikTok account is connected before you publish.
  • To upload your videos (video.upload) and publish them with the settings you chose (video.publish).
  • To show your recent videos and account statistics so you can confirm that a post went live.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties other than TikTok, which receives your videos and settings when you publish.

3. Storage and retention

Tokens and account details are stored on the server that runs the Service and are used only for the purposes above. Uploaded video files are kept only as long as needed to publish them. Account data is deleted when you disconnect your TikTok account from the Service or ask us to delete it.

4. Your choices and rights

You can revoke Uploadz's access at any time in the TikTok app ("Settings and privacy", "Security and permissions", "Apps and services"). You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, including under Brazil's LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) and, where it applies, the EU GDPR.

5. Security

We use HTTPS to talk to TikTok and restrict access to stored tokens. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18, or under the minimum age TikTok requires in your country.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
